Universal Commerce Protocol

Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) is an open standard for commerce interoperability, co-developed by industry leaders including Google. It gives platforms, agents, and businesses a common way to discover capabilities and transact without bespoke integrations.

Businesses publish a profile at /.well-known/ucp, platforms advertise their own profile on each request, and the two sides negotiate compatible services, capabilities, and transports.
